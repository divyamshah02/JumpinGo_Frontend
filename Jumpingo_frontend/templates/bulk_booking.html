Frequently Asked Questions

Yes! Schools, colleges, and corporate teams can reserve exclusive private slots during morning or afternoon hours so your group enjoys unrestricted access to all inflatable zones without public queues.

You can host your group at any of our active festival grounds across Gujarat (including Ahmedabad, Surat, and Mehsana). For large corporate family days, society carnivals, or college fests, we also offer custom on-site inflatable setups at your private venue.

Yes. All corporate and institutional bookings include 100% GST-compliant invoicing, along with any required vendor onboarding documents, safety certifications, and formal quotations for internal approvals.

We offer flexible F&B add-ons ranging from boxed kid-friendly snack combos and hydration stations to full multi-course corporate buffets. Outside catering is not permitted inside.

Safety is our top priority. Every bulk booking includes age-segregated play zones, mandatory grip socks for all participants, an on-site first-aid station, and a strict, trained Safety Marshals monitoring all inflatables at all times.

We understand group headcounts can fluctuate. You can confirm your final guest count up to 72 hours before your event, and bookings can be rescheduled once free of charge if notified at least 7 days in advance (or in the event of severe weather conditions).
